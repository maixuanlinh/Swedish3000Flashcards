DOWNLOAD FOR OFFLINE STUDY
Your 211 Free A-set lessons download automatically over Wi-Fi or mobile data. Premium can download the complete collection, choose Wi-Fi only, pause, continue or remove downloads. Notifications require permission and Android may delay alerts according to battery and notification settings. Opening a reminder does not mark a word as Known.
