Build your Finnish vocabulary, one memorable word at a time.

3000 Finnish Words combines English–Finnish flashcards with illustrations, memory hooks and example sentences. Practise at your own pace and keep track of the words you know.

START FREE
• Study all 3,000 basic English–Finnish word pairs.
• Hear Finnish word pronunciation with an installed offline voice.
• Try 211 complete A-set lessons, including illustrations, memory hooks, Finnish examples, English translations and automatic study.
• Mark words as New, Learning or Known, and follow your progress.
• Set optional local word reminders for any letter.

REMEMBER WITH THE FULL PICTURE
An optional one-time Premium purchase unlocks complete lessons across all 3,000 words. Explore illustrated memory cues, examples with English translations, sentence pronunciation and automatic playback for any letter or selection. No subscription or advertising.

MAKE PRACTICE YOUR OWN
• Switch between English-first and Finnish-first cards.
• Search the collection and filter by learning status, English starting letter or topic.
• Swipe or use the rating buttons, undo a rating and shuffle the remaining cards.
• Adjust automatic-study pauses, audio options and voice speed.
• Add your own artwork to accessible complete lessons and keep earlier images and prompts in history.

AVAILABLE OFFLINE
Vocabulary and illustrations are delivered with installation. Pronunciation requires suitable offline speech voices installed on your device. Google Play is needed to purchase or restore Premium. Android may delay reminders according to battery and notification settings.

Your learning progress and imported artwork stay in this app on your device. Restoring a purchase restores lesson access, not your learning history. Apple and Android purchases are separate.
