Privacy policy

3000 Swedish Words is an independent learning app provided by Linapps. Updated 2 November 2026.

Learning stays on your device

The app stores learning ratings, review history, settings, reminder schedules, downloaded lessons and artwork you choose to import on your device. It does not require an app account and does not upload your learning history or imported artwork to us. On iOS, device backups are controlled by your Apple settings. The Android app disables automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup of its private learning data.

Lesson downloads

The app retrieves lesson pictures, recorded pronunciation and a media manifest from yki.essaykingapp.com, hosted through Bluehost. The server necessarily receives technical information such as your IP address, request time, requested file path and standard network request information. Hosting security and access logs may retain this information to operate and protect the service. We do not use these requests to build advertising profiles or track you across apps. Downloaded lessons are cached locally for offline use.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. The app uses StoreKit to verify whether Premium is available to your Apple Account and restore purchases. We do not receive your card details. Apple handles its own purchase records under its privacy policy.

On Android, Google Play processes the optional one-time Premium purchase. The app uses the Google Play Billing Library to obtain product details, verify signed purchase records, acknowledge completed purchases and restore access. A verified signed purchase record is kept locally so access can remain available offline. We do not receive card details. Google handles its payment services under its privacy policy. Apple and Android purchases are separate.

No advertising or analytics SDKs

The app includes no advertising or third-party behavioural analytics SDKs. It does not request microphone access or record your voice.

Optional actions

Importing artwork uses the system photo or file picker; only files you select are used locally. Reminders require notification permission and are scheduled on the device. If you manually open an external service such as ChatGPT, that service handles information you choose to share under its own policy. The app does not automatically send your word history to it.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use it to respond and troubleshoot, and retain it only as needed for support and applicable record-keeping requirements. Do not send payment credentials or sensitive personal information.

Your choices

You can remove offline media in the app, change notification permissions in your device settings, and delete local app data by deleting the app. To ask about support messages you have sent, contact support@essaykingapp.com. Purchase records maintained by Apple or Google are managed through the corresponding platform account.

Children

The app has no social features or account registration and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children. A parent or guardian can contact support with a privacy concern.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
