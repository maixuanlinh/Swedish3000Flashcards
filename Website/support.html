Help with 3000 Swedish Words

Email support@essaykingapp.com. Include your app version, device model, Android or iOS version, and the word or screen involved. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

What is free?

All 3,000 word pairs, learning ratings and word pronunciation, plus all 211 complete lessons in the English A set. Premium is an optional one-time purchase for the remaining illustrated lessons, examples, sentence audio and unrestricted automatic study.

Restore a purchase

Use the Apple Account or Google Play account that purchased Premium on that platform, then choose Restore Purchases on the Premium offer or Progress screen. Apple and Android purchases are separate. Restoring access does not restore your learning history or downloaded files.

Offline pictures and sound

Starter media is included. Other media needs an internet connection the first time. In Progress → Offline study, download the lessons you want before travelling. Allow enough storage and on iOS, keep the app open while downloading; on Android, the system can continue downloads in the background. The complete optional media library is approximately 657 MB including starter media. Downloaded lessons stay on your device until removed.

Learning progress and reminders

Ratings, review history, preferences and imported pictures are stored locally. There is no app account or cross-device sync. Deleting the app can remove these records. Reminders are optional local notifications; change permission in your device settings.

Report a card or audio issue

Send the English and Swedish word and describe the issue, including whether it concerns spelling, meaning, scene or pronunciation. A screenshot is helpful if you choose to include one.
